Become an Ayna tester

Ayna turns an Android TV / Google TV into a wireless display for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows (AirPlay, Miracast, DLNA). Before its Google Play launch we need real TV users for a 14-day closed test.

You need: an Android TV / Google TV device on Android 11 or newer (TV, Mi Box, Chromecast with Google TV, TV box…) and the Google account you use on it.
  1. Join the group with the Google account of your TV.
    Join ayna-test
  2. Become a tester: tap "Become a tester" on the page that opens.
    Join the test
  3. Install on your TV: search "Ayna" in the Play Store on the TV. It can take a few hours to show up after you join.
  4. Stay for 14 days: keep the app installed and stay in the group and the test. Try mirroring a few times.

Bugs or ideas: krmndigital@gmail.com. As a thank-you, every tester gets a lifetime Ayna Pro code after launch. No rating or review is required for it.

If the test link says the app isn't available, Google's review hasn't finished yet: join the group and try again in a few days.
